Privacy Policy

Compliant with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 & GDPR Standards.
Effective Date:

BENEFITZ INTERNATIONAL ("we", "us", or "our"), headquartered in Dubai, UAE, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our global migration consultancy services.

By accessing our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. We adhere strictly to the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and international data protection standards applicable to cross-border immigration services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data necessary to process your residency or citizenship applications and assess eligibility:

  • Personal Identification: Name, Passport Details, Date of Birth, Nationality, Gender.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, Email address, Physical address.
  • Professional Information: CV/Resume, Employment History, Education Certificates, Business Interests.
  • Financial Information: Documentation required for Proof of Funds or Investment programs.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage data via Cookies (see Section 7).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used for specific, lawful purposes defined by your service agreement with us:

  • To assess your eligibility for various global mobility and residency programs.
  • To prepare and submit applications to relevant government departments and authorities.
  • To facilitate processing through authorized partners and visa centers.
  • To manage client communications and provide status updates on pending files.
  • To maintain internal compliance with UAE and international regulatory standards.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. However, processing global migration files requires sharing information with:

  • Government Authorities: Relevant immigration and security departments in destination countries.
  • Legal Partners: Licensed attorneys or authorized agents required for specific program filings.
  • Financial Institutions: Banks or investment funds involved in citizenship-by-investment or residency-by-investment routes.
  • Technical Providers: Secure IT infrastructures and cloud platforms used for data storage and management.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and restricted access protocols. Physical documents are stored in secure archives at our Dubai corporate office.

While we strive to use the most advanced commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights (UAE & GDPR)

Under UAE Data Law and GDPR (where applicable), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data held by us.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of data ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to legal retention requirements for governmental applications.
  • Portability: Request the transfer of your data to another organization.
