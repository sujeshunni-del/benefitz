Who We Are

Executive Summary

Benefitz International is a specialized global mobility consultancy serving as the premier strategic partner for comprehensive immigration, residency, and citizenship solutions. Headquartered in Dubai, UAE, our expertise spans key economic regions including Canada, Europe (Schengen), Australia, the UK, the USA, and New Zealand.

We meticulously orchestrate complex processes—from initial needs assessment and employment authorization to high-net-worth investment migration. Our mission is to ensure a secure, seamless, and fully compliant long-term transition for every client.
